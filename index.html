About

I got into engineering through robotics. At fifteen I joined my school’s FRC team, and I’ve liked the problems where software meets physics ever since: motors that saturate, sensors that lie and code that has milliseconds to react.

Outside the lab I’m Vice President and HR Manager of ESN Twente, the student association for international students. Next, I want to work on safety-critical embedded systems and do a master’s in Embedded Systems.

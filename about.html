About

I got into engineering through robotics. At fifteen I joined my school’s FRC team and ended up designing the drive system for our competition robot. It taught me that the interesting problems live where software meets physics, and that’s still what I want to work on.

I chose Technical Computer Science at Twente because it lets me go deep on both sides. So far I’ve written a stack-based processor in Clash, deployed a pruned and quantised YOLO model on a Hailo NPU, and scheduled a safety-critical cabin monitoring system with Rate Monotonic analysis. This year my minor adds circuits, sensors and the modelling and control of a real wheeled robot.

Outside the lab I mostly work with people instead of machines. I think the two belong together: the best engineers I’ve met are also the ones who can keep a team together when things go wrong.

Next, I want to work on safety-critical embedded systems, ideally in the defence and aerospace sector, and do a master’s in Embedded Systems.
